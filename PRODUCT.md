# Product

## Register

product

## Purpose

GoMeta Pro is a SaaS platform that helps Vietnamese SME advertisers manage, automate, and optimize their Facebook/Meta Ads campaigns. It replaces manual work in Meta Ads Manager with AI-driven automation — from targeting analysis to campaign creation to performance monitoring.

## Users

- **Primary**: Vietnamese SME business owners and marketing staff who run Facebook Ads (restaurants, hotels, e-commerce, local services)
- **Context**: Desktop-first, used during work hours, often by people who are NOT ads experts but need to manage campaigns daily
- **Job to be done**: Create effective ad campaigns quickly, monitor spend/performance, automate repetitive tasks (pause/resume by schedule, rules-based optimization)
- **Skill level**: Intermediate — familiar with Facebook but overwhelmed by Ads Manager complexity

## Brand Personality

Professional, Intelligent, Trustworthy

The platform should feel like having a smart ads expert working alongside you — competent and reliable, not flashy or toy-like. Users trust it with real ad budgets.

## References

- **Facebook Ads Manager**: Industry standard, familiar patterns for advertisers. Specifically: the campaign/adset/ad hierarchy, data tables with metrics, date range selectors
- **What to take**: Table layouts, metric card patterns, filter/action bar placement, professional color usage

## Anti-references

- **Terminal/hacker dark themes**: Too extreme, hard to read, alienates non-technical users
- **Canva-style playful UI**: Too simple, lacks professional credibility for a tool managing real ad budgets
- **Avoid**: Excessive animations, toy-like rounded elements, overly colorful dashboards that distract from data

## Design Principles

1. **Data clarity first**: Every screen exists to help users understand and act on their ad data. Don't obscure numbers with decoration
2. **Progressive disclosure**: Show essential info upfront, advanced options on demand. Don't overwhelm with all options at once
3. **Confidence through feedback**: Every action (create, pause, activate) should have clear confirmation and status. Users are spending real money
4. **Familiar patterns**: Follow Meta Ads Manager conventions where it makes sense — users already know those patterns

## Accessibility

- WCAG AA compliance minimum
- Support for color-blind users (don't rely solely on red/green for status)
- Readable font sizes (min 13px for data, 11px for labels)
- Keyboard navigable for power users
